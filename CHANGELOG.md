# Changelog

## 0.1.21 - 2026-10-29

- Update call status from events instead of polling.
- Confirm call audio on the first packet after acceptance, without ongoing interruption checks.
- Wait for quick-reply audio readiness using events.

## 0.1.20 - 2026-10-27

Initial public release.

- WP-2MED terminal pairing and cloud connection.
- Live video, cached snapshots, ringing events, and two-way audio.
- Optional unlock, quick reply, and ringing sensor accessories for HomeKit.
- Bundled Japanese delivery reply with microphone takeover and restoration.
- Experimental status video with OSD and configurable auto answer on live view.
- English settings, status messages, and diagnostics.
